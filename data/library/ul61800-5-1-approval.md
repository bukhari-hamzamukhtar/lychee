## Clause 4.4 — reinforced insulation components

Components providing reinforced or double insulation between hazardous-voltage
circuits and operator-accessible circuits are safety-critical. Substitution
requires re-evaluation of the insulation coordination for every product
containing the part, and the certification body must witness the dielectric
tests.

Components designated critical under this clause:

| Part | Insulation role |
|---|---|
| ADUM1201ARZ | Reinforced isolation, control to power stage |
| MKP1848C61050JY5 | DC-link capacitor, basic insulation to chassis |

## Clause 4.4.3 — acceptance criteria for a substitute

A substitute must be evidenced against all of:

1. Equal or greater rated isolation voltage, with the manufacturer's own
   certification to a recognised insulation standard.
2. Equal or greater pollution-degree and material-group ratings.
3. Equal or greater partial-discharge performance where the original was
   qualified for it.
4. Dielectric withstand re-tested on three production samples per product.

A supplier datasheet claiming equivalence is not sufficient on its own. The
substitute's own certification must be on file.

## Clause 5.2 — end-of-life components

Where a safety-critical component is discontinued, the approval remains valid
for units already built and for units built from stock purchased before the
supplier's last-time-buy date. It does **not** extend to units built from a
substituted part, whatever the electrical equivalence.

Stock purchased under a last-time-buy therefore carries the existing approval
for its whole life. This is the central reason a last-time-buy is usually
cheaper than a substitution for a part named in this clause.

## Clause 6.1 — records

The approval file records the manufacturer, part number and date code range of
every component named above. A last-time-buy that spans several date codes must
record the full range, and the incoming inspection record must be retained for
the contracted service life.
